# 18a — Frozen voice candidate process repeatability

Status: bounded prerequisite verified. Dependency: [18](18-voice-reproduction.md).
[Evidence](../assets/18a-voice-repeatability/README.md).

## Contract

Before porting the frozen reference-conditioned candidate into a managed worker,
verify that the unchanged local runtime and fixed requests still reproduce its
complete output bytes across fresh processes. Compare request, source, runtime,
dependencies and model identities before generation. Keep the accepted candidate
and failed alternatives untouched; do not change seeds or repin outputs on failure.

The experiment permits at most two fresh offline executions of the unchanged
slice 18 runner. Compare complete generated PCM and complete files against the
frozen outputs and each other, including the actual extracted reference bytes.
A mismatch banks a bounded failure rather than starting a quality sweep.

## Result and boundary

Both processes match every frozen word and phrase output exactly. This establishes
a repeatable baseline for a production-intended entry parity gate on these fixed
cases and this environment. It does not guarantee determinism for arbitrary
inputs/hardware, establish managed reference origins, accept voice/splice quality,
or enable `voice.generate`. The three origin labels remain copies of one reference.

Fresh process does not mean cold system/Metal caches. Save generated bytes for
replay; a seed is not a durable output. The next entry-port proposal must preserve
the frozen recipe and prove byte parity before adding public durable job/asset
behavior. Listening and ownership acceptance remain with slices 18 and 19.
