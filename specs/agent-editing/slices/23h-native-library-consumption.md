## Contract and owner

Native library controls list caller-created projects and route a person's explicit
preview, export or deletion request through the existing public operations and
controllers. Capture rows expose actual source facts/admission and deletion.
Capture creates no project, association, layout or editing settings. Listing and
delivery controls are consumer operations; editing remains with the external caller.

Use one library-consumer boundary for the asynchronous reads, paging and deletion
currently embedded in RecordingControls. Reuse MediaTarget for shared identity and
the existing preview/export controllers. Preserve capture status cadence and
aggregate storage ownership; project navigation cannot delay either through an
unbounded scan. Reads do not submit jobs, retry admission or trigger speech processing.

The service's project list is ordered by ascending creation sequence. Present an
honestly named Projects page with bounded explicit navigation and preserve its
returned order/cursor. Do not scan all projects to manufacture recency. Failed or
malformed reads retain last-good content with a visible failure. Delayed responses
cannot restore a deleted target or replace a page/service generation that changed.

Fresh capture rows have no composition revision: source duration alone does not
make recording preview/export available. Use actual source-admission receipts and
bounded job reads to distinguish pending publication/admission, refusal and job
progress. Do not query unsupported recording processing/index operations for these
rows or imply that capture automatically transcribes. Preserve installed recording
composition behavior through its actual revision identity until the hard cutover;
never fabricate a project to keep an action enabled.

Deletion retains the exact typed target across an unanswered request and discovery
that hides a fenced owner. A retry acts on that same identity. Close that target's
preview when deletion starts; validate the receipt before forgetting only its
export state. Recording/project IDs
with the same text remain separate. Ready acquisitions and their projects survive
recording deletion; the native consumer adds no cascading deletion policy.

## Observation lifetime

Last-good processing observations are retained only for their exact recording
owner. Selecting a newer take or replacing the service clears that context; a
failed read cannot describe the previous take as the newest one. A pending job
read also loses authority when a new recording catalog request begins, so its late
answer cannot clear another take's read failure. These are consumer observation
fences, not additional preparation or recording lifecycles.
