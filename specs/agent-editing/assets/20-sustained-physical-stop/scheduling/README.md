Status: controlled native lifecycle contract verified at `3d2ed80d`. Once inputs
are closed, a usable camera or primary source no longer waits for its sibling's
publication. Stop still joins both before releasing journal authority, and retry
keeps each source's original validation and error precedence.

reports append to the live take journal. Those later reports may grow the live
journal; they cannot change the published source's frozen prefix. An existing
receipt with missing or substituted authority refuses rather than reconstructing
the snapshot.
