# Scoped rendering acceptance

The selected balanced H.264 settings are accepted for the tested temporal source
membership and static layer/pointer composition contracts in slices06 and15.
This is an explicit quality judgment under the existing allowance for documented
codec error, not a relaxation of the exact pre-encode or clock checks.

The [complete capture](README.md) preserves exact frozen source/program/pointer
inputs, all595 decoded frames and their actual packet clocks, matched non-grid
full/range selections, and byte-identical full preview/export pairs. Earlier
[geometry acceptance evidence](../15-acceptance-audit/README.md) covers two canvases,
all fit modes, orientation, nested alpha, source replacement, history, output-stage
processing and protected narration. The [maintenance audit](maintenance-audit.md)
reconciles these proofs with the named contracts rather than restarting completed
geometry work.

The [fresh visual review](visual-review.md) observes softer thin trails/grid lines
and rougher small text/card edges in encoded output. It does not identify displaced
landmarks or a missing positive-cohort pointer; deliberately shifted and removed
controls remain clearly distinguishable. Root inspected the corrected small full
contexts/pointer crops and recorded frame20 context/detail sheets and agrees with
that scoped assessment. Fine-detail loss is accepted for this reversible balanced
preset; agents retain explicit controls when another tradeoff is desired.

Historical four-code RGB, thin-magenta and compressed-byte diagnostics remain
failed where recorded. None was established as a universal user requirement.
They remain useful loss measurements; they do not become passing tests or override
exact source selection, geometry, support, phase or time. The inconsistent black
standalone-image observation remains documented as a presentation limitation:
original positive images are byte-identical and contain the same opaque raster.

Color admission preserves platform-decoded interpretation of untagged media and
refuses unsupported declared HDR/wide-gamut/custom profiles without an explicit
conversion. It does not claim to recover an untagged source author's unknown
intent. This policy is implemented by the shared native color admission owner
and covered by the [production video evidence](../07-video/README.md).

This disposition does not establish continuous movie playback, speech/listening,
animated-geometry appearance, all camera/source formats, scale or full-editor
acceptance. Slice16 retains animated full/range verification, delivered retime/gain
and denoise-transition requirements; their owners remain open. No codec, matte,
bitrate sweep or numerical threshold change is introduced by this decision.

A [fresh read-only acceptance audit](acceptance-review.log.gz) found no concrete
unmet requirement invalidating the scoped06/15 closure. It inspected retained
evidence and contracts; it did not rerun media or assess playback/listening.
