# Recording-package choices

- Use one native source-admission helper for project and recording consumers. Keep domain-specific binding reconstruction and ordered page comparison in their respective owners; no TypeScript clock or second journal parser.
- Include only publication receipts named by verified source evidence in the existing archive inventory. Missing or downgraded proof cannot be inferred from a MOV filename.
- Verify both raw normalized evidence and the pages actually consumed by audio reads. Streaming comparisons preserve the recording domain without importing the acquisition owner's in-memory interval cap.
- Reuse caller-owned temporary workspaces and inherit their existing lock into native children. Inspection passes a borrowed lifetime descriptor; it does not transfer or duplicate lifetime ownership.
- Preserve legacy source normalization and existing per-call deadlines. This checkpoint makes no full-scale deadline or new input-cap claim.
