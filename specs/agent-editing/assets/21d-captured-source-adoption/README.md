The camera publication owner verifies complete ordered pictures, exact native
presentation times and bounded support against its retained mapping. Fresh
`acquisition.import` adopts that verified source through existing jobs/assets and
source evidence; the camera remains an independent acquisition with ordinary
video role. One publication-member inventory also retains its receipt/mapping
through donor deletion and portable project relocation. Plain `asset.import`
keeps physical media scope and does not manufacture capture provenance.

[Public verification](public-verification.json) exercises supplied take/source/device
identity, exact source-to-asset support, missing/changed proof and clock conflicts,
operational retry, a lost native response across restart, request replay and
relocation after original-library/donor/archive deletion. The fixture explicitly
authors its project. These supplied identities do not establish allocation by a
fresh capture service; [21f](../../slices/21f-public-camera-selection.md) owns that
lifecycle work. This pass adds no public camera-start selector or authoring setting.

The [native packet](evidence.tar.gz) contains actual shared owner controls for
journal prefixes, canonical descriptor path replacement, same-byte local mapping,
receipt mutation on publication retry, bounded mapping growth and historical raw
scale authority. Its fractional prefix ends at exactly 200000/3 microseconds,
strictly inside the final mapping's nominal interval ending 100000; both recovery
and source evidence retain that exact physical endpoint. The current-source
preserved 21b audio-retry source is included for later caller-authored integration,
with the original unbound probe identity rather than a fabricated allocation.

[Retained verification](retained-verification.json) passes the actual saved iPhone
canonical movie, original journal and observation bytes through the same read-only
verifier once, preserving all 4,428 represented pictures and their recorded digest.
It uses an exact same-byte private raw clone solely for the old receipt's missing
native digest timescale. Removing that clone truthfully refuses raw-less proof
before picture verification; no receipt rewrite or new publication is performed.
The original recovered receipt remains unbound, and its partial diagnostics remain
historical evidence. Required identity/picture reads are disclosed in the result;
this is not another publication or timing cohort.

A camera receipt's pre-terminal journal prefix binds header identity, shared
origin and pause facts. Later terminal rows may be appended, so the prefix does
not attest their state/duration payload. Fresh admission independently retains
and inventories the complete source journal. New bound production camera proof
requires its own identity, prefix, native timescale and exact support; historical
unbound proof is never silently upgraded.
