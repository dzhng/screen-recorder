# Public journey decisions

Accepted: reuse the authored cases and independent inverse-coordinate oracle.
The public runner owns transport, revision and delivery checks; it does not
reimplement fitting or learn expected geometry from compiled/native receipts.

Accepted: exercise a representative public subset of the larger native matrix,
with complete processing taps for nested stacks. This accepts static layer
transport and lifetime behavior, not every remaining edit-operation journey or
pointer/animation feature.

Accepted: verify fractional ranges through preview and full revisions through
export, matching the actual API. Export copies must equal independently checked
preview bytes. Geometric movie checks and measured codec errors remain separate;
lossless WAV assertions do not claim lossless AAC delivery or close codec quality.

Accepted: preserve committed external exports after project deletion while
revoking service-owned delivery tokens. Re-render retained assets through a new
project to establish ownership beyond catalog-row survival.
