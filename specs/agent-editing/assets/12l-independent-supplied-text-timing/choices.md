# Reviewed choices

## Independent phase children — sound, high confidence

The format bridge, finite conversion and aligner each own one process. The bridge
first publishes a completed file and exits; only after complete sample equality
passes does conversion start. The aligner likewise starts only after the converter
has closed and its complete header, receipt and sample clock agree. A shared
worker could keep the two native calls in one lifetime, but it would not improve
this output question and would blur their independent bounds and terminal records.
This choice reuses 12i's subprocess/wait4 observation pattern without introducing a
production lifecycle mechanism. Three actual zero terminals support the claimed
scope; they establish no speed benefit.

The recipe, exact complete source interval, Int16/32768 equality, thresholds,
private licensed storage, resource criterion and fail-stop/no-retry rules were
explicit root decisions. The current helper and two precision restorations are
also explicit; the copies are frozen case evidence, not another maintained owner.
There is no uncertain product decision. Historical binary identity and ASR PCM
equality remain evidential limits, not choices silently filled by substitutes.
