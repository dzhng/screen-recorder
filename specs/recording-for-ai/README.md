# Original recording design and evidence

This directory retains the original recording implementation record. The toolkit
has shipped; the [release disposition](../done/agent-editing/release-closeout.md)
owns its accepted limitations. Historical unchecked items are unverified observations,
not an active queue or a request for another recording.

## Read this as a historical record

[Discovery](MAP.md), [decisions](choices.md) and [research](research.md) explain the
original choices. [Architecture](architecture.md), [contracts](contracts.md) and
[verification](verification.md) describe the implementation and acceptance model
at that stage. The [slice source](slices/) owns the original build ladder and its
individual evidence links; the [release snapshot](https://github.com/dzhng/screen-recorder/tree/c08e0bd8e10903d482232818ccfffaf1d653555a/specs/recording-for-ai)
retains the full handoff and checklists.
The [evidence directory](assets/) retains original observations and producer records.

Some recording-index, timeline and package owners were replaced. Their original
commands and diagrams must not guide new implementation. Current ownership and
product boundaries live in the [root guide](../../README.md), and the
[preservation registry](../done/agent-editing/assets/23-owner-fixture-ports/README.md)
locates source/project proof carried through those changes.

## Why retain the evidence

Capture, recovery and geometry observations depend on actual platform inputs.
Speech and cuts depend on independent audible labels. A saved controlled fixture
cannot stand in for either, and a later implementation cannot silently convert an
original failure into a pass.

Original fixtures and journals remain reusable. Concrete defects return to their
current owner with focused verification. Consult [current verification tools](../../packages/test-harness/README.md)
for reproduction instead of rebuilding a retired library from an old plan.
