## Decisions from this planning conversation

[MAP.md](MAP.md) owns user attribution and rationales. The accepted complete scope
includes reliability, autonomous QA, multicam, speaker labeling, captions, transitions, grading and
focused skill references. Capability discovery is the first internal editing step.
Available tools are used; absent tools are recommended only when useful/required.
The corpus target is about 250 MB with larger media in LFS, subject to fidelity.
Managed data may reset under a hard cutover with no shims/aliases/migrations.
Original external media and historical evidence remain intact. This turn plans
implementation; it does not authorize a new trailer-editing project.

The earlier teaser request survives in 33: when deriving a teaser/episode intro
from a complete interview, a complete spicy question can be the ending while its
answer is withheld. Full highlights keep the answer and qualifications. Existing
use-case techniques are preserved and split into focused files rather than replaced
with a universal teaser template.

The [retro](retro.md) owns session-specific attribution/limits. Its work-multiplication
table maps import/replay, helper provenance, transcription, inconsistent replies,
speech checking, reference interpretation, geometry, audio, exports, QA coverage and
reproduction to the slices above. Retained excerpts are selected evidence, not a
claim to have replayed every tool call or viewed the historical final videos.

Speech timing checkpoint [07](assets/07-speech-timing/README.md) now preserves
raw overlapping and instant observations. Its paired bounded source/derivative
receipts certify the two overlap corpus inputs without implying independent
lexical truth or whole-corpus completion. Root integration rebuilt the affected
package owners and passed 68 reader/seed/project checks plus 27 consumer checks.
