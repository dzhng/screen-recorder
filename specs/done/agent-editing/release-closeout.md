# Release disposition

The user accepted delivery with remaining product review and measurement limits.
This record closes the build plan; it does not turn missing physical or perceptual
observations into passing results. Current packaging and versioned releases belong
to the [build/release guide](../../../scripts/README.md).

The [personal installation receipt](release.json) records the installed bundle,
signature and runtime at closeout. It is a host-specific installation observation,
separate from a portable tagged package or a later working-tree change. Capture
and model preparation remain explicit actions.

The [preservation registry](assets/23-owner-fixture-ports/README.md) and
[capability receipt](assets/acceptance-maintenance/native-capability-verification.json)
retain implementation-end results and focused recoveries. Exact identities and
check counts belong to those records, not another progress checklist.

## Accepted limitations

- Speech remains best effort. Broader independent disfluency labels, held-out
  timing and protected joins are unverified; omissions and numerical failures remain.
- Retained screen/camera inputs meet the requested take's purpose, but their marker
  evidence does not establish the physical synchronization bound or all device-loss
  and staggered-start behavior.
- Scoped Stop, retry and playback observations do not establish every lifecycle,
  uninterrupted perceptual quality or a general performance guarantee.
- Sampled visuals and muted endpoint playback do not provide complete listening.
- Isolated storage passes do not resolve the original deadline under heavy concurrent
  compilation. The historical repository run was not fully green.

These limits do not authorize hidden corrections, relaxed thresholds or destruction
of originals. Concrete defects return to their existing owner with focused proof.
The [acceptance disposition](assets/acceptance-maintenance/release-gates.md) binds
observations to retained evidence.
