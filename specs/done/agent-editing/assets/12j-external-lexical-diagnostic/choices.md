  The first complete source prefix reaching forty labels costs 55 utterances and
  365 seconds of audio. Selecting source-order utterances with at least two labels,

  fragment spellings remain ambiguous. Automatic alignments are not word-edge truth,

  occurrence attribution. The plan did not specify this measurement entry point.
  It introduces only harness exports and counts, with no product policy or new
  model, dependency, storage schema or editing operation.
