No concrete workflow error is visible in the inspected requests/receipts.

- Placement: full images consistently show black above and left, footage beginning at (32,16), and footage extending to the right/bottom canvas edges. This agrees with full-size translation and intentional overflow clipping; no unintended scaling or pointer detachment is visible. High confidence, full-frame evidence.

Exact 20% is established by returned settings, not inferred from display brightness. High confidence, full and crop evidence.
- Split continuity: Case 1's 1.0 s cross-version pair and Case 2's same-brightness 1.0/1.1 s pairs retain trail shape and position. No visible restart or seam artifact. High confidence for these samples only, full and crop evidence.
- Detail: crops reveal stepped cursor edges, dark outline/fringe and small segmented/soft sections in the thin pink trail. These appear consistently in split and unsplit samples and are expected at this small raster size/enlargement; I cannot substantiate a new rendering defect from them. No clipped cursor, extra pointer, detached trail or abnormal block artifact is visible. Moderate-to-high confidence, primarily crop evidence.

## Skill clarification

A short procedural clarification would help: before composing a processor stack, check both execution and allowed targets in processing.capabilities; keep an acquisition-backed pointer on an allowed clip, insert footage-only opacity before that pointer, and preserve the existing pointer step ID when retaining the effect. Case 2's invalid track request and unnecessary pointer replacement are concrete evidence for this reminder. The existing skill already says to check capabilities and retain step IDs, so this is targeted clarification, not a missing fundamental workflow or justification for a broad rewrite. Preserve a rejected write's raw receipt separately when retrying so recovery can be audited. Neither case supports blocking delivery or demanding full playback beyond the honestly disclosed verification.
