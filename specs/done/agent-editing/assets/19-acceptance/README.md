room-tone verdicts. Its final choice selects the200ms overlap treatment:
**speech-free, with a slight residual seam explicitly tolerated**. The50ms loop
and250ms whirling comparison remain rejected evidence. No perfectly seamless

listening claim for its particular edit. Its proof combines with the actual
accepted contextual auditions and selected loop; unchanged voice output is not
regenerated merely to repeat quality checks. Historic reports retain their
creation-time pending dispositions and limitations, while the current user
record supplies later quality acceptance.

retain their own exact identities; agents still choose placement, gain and
