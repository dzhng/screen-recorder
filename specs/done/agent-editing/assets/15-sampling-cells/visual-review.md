# Independent visual review

A separate agent inspected all 124 native layer captures, all 321 public
layer/pointer images (184 independently hash-verified unique files), six source
controls and eight recorded ProRes frames. Full pictures and enlarged crops were
included, with blank/transparent states retained. Fresh-agent creation was twice
rejected by the concurrency limit, so the reviewer was an agent from the unrelated
noise lane. It knew that video encoding was under investigation; descriptive
red/green filenames could suggest an outcome. This is a limited-context
independent review, not a fully blind review.

The reviewer saw softened/brightened perimeter pixels in the red identity output
and a more faithful green perimeter. Recorded frames remained readable, with no
obvious new edge seam, crop or color defect; visual inspection could not establish
numerical identity or confidently rank the recorded variants. Small transformed
pointers were soft but remained attached to their strokes. The supplied source
was itself small and rasterized; no erroneous additional blur was established.

The initial review flagged a thin gray line above the presenter. Preserve that
finding in [its crop](presenter-line.png). The subsequent
[screen-only control](screen-only-line-control.png) has exactly the same pixels
above the presenter's authored top edge at y24, including that line. The
[presenter control](presenter-line-control.png) and [measurements](line-control.json)
resolve it as the exposed sampled edge of the underlying screen marker, not a
new fragment. The reviewer agreed after inspecting the controls; that resolution
is explicitly informed by geometry context.

No remaining actionable visible defect was identified in the supplied set.
Exact alpha/color, numerical geometry, and motion between stills remain outside
this visual verdict; their independent tests and open gates are recorded separately.
